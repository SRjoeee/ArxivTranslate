## 2026-09-13 — one way to wait for the chain after a save (INVENTORY P4)

- Branch `rebuild/chain-fresh` (PR #182). The popup restarted a page on new settings after polling `axt:provider-status` ten times with a field predicate of its own choosing (`shared/chain.ts`, deleted); the options page's `axt:engine-ready` was the other path. - Tests 1 634 (+4); five mutation checks; gate green by exit code; `pnpm e2e` 72/72.
